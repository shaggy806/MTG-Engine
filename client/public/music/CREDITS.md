# Music

The background music in a game, played by `client/src/game/music.ts` (its `TRACKS` list). All by
[RandomMind](https://opengameart.org/users/randommind) on OpenGameArt, all CC0. Re-encoded from the
originals (192–320 kbps MP3) to 96 kbps MP3 and normalised to -23 LUFS, so `music.ts` sets one
level for all of them.
